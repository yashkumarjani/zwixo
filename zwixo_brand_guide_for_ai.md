## 1. Brand Identity & core concept
* **Brand Name:** ZWIXO (pronounced *Zwi-xo*)
* **Tagline:** Snap it. Glow it. Deliver it.
* **Core Value Proposition:** ZWIXO is a premium, WhatsApp-first Memory & Celebration Studio run by designer Gautami in Ahmedabad, India. We professionally restore vintage family photographs, design customized wedding countdown posters, and curate bespoke baby milestone cards.
* **The ZWIXO USP (Unique Selling Proposition):** 
  * **No Generic Templates:** Unlike automated apps, everything is manually hand-crafted by professional designers.
  * **WhatsApp-First UX:** 100% friction-free. Customers order, share photos, review drafts, and get final HD files delivered in 30 seconds directly on WhatsApp.
  * **No AI Distortion:** In photo restorations, facial reconstructions are done manually with high detailing, keeping the emotions authentic without weird AI-looking distortions.
  * **Fast Turnaround:** Deliveries within 24 to 48 hours.

## 2. Brand Visuals & Aesthetic Language
When planning posters, visuals, or video storyboards, strictly follow these visual rules:
* **Primary Color Palette:** 
  * **Saffron Gold (`#F5A623`):** Represents warmth, Indian celebrations, premium quality, and glow.
  * **Deep Ink Dark (`#1C1C1E`):** Represents elegance, contrast, clean modern typography, and high-end photography.
  * **White/Warm Light Grays:** Used to keep layouts clean and breathable.
* **Mood & Visual Style:** Glassmorphic overlays, clean split-screens (Before/After), high-contrast layouts, warm ambient glows, and minimalist modern typography (Sans-serif, clean letter-spacing).
* **Key Visual Metaphor:** A simulated WhatsApp chat bubble showing real designer-to-customer interactions (e.g., *"Here is your draft, Priya! Let me know if you need any changes."* followed by a heart emoji).

## 6. Sample Frameworks for AI Prompt Tasks
*When you (the AI) are asked to create tasks, follow these structures:*
* **For Posters:** Describe the layout in a 2x2 grid or split-screen format. Specify colors (Saffron and Ink), specify exact headlines, and explain the visual focus (e.g. "Focus on the emotion in the eyes").
* **For Reels Storyboards:** Structure it in seconds (0s - 3s Hook, 3s - 10s Problem/Process, 10s - 15s Emotional Reward & CTA). Suggest background audio styles (flute, emotional lo-fi, ambient beats).
* **For Ad Copy:** Provide a **Hook** (question or statement), a **Core Problem/Solution**, a **USPs Checklist**, and a **Direct WhatsApp Link CTA**.
